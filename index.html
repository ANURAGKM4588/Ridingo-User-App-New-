01 SERVICE NATURE
✓ Verified

1. Service Description

Ridingo operates strictly as a peer-to-peer driver-dispatch and technology platform connecting registered private car owners with verified freelance drivers under the "Your car. Our driver." model.

  • We provide professional driving assistance for your personal vehicle. Ridingo does not own commercial transport fleets, cabs, or yellow-board taxis.
  • The vehicle owner retains full legal custody, care, and ownership of their vehicle throughout the trip.
02 BOOKING & RATES
✓ Standard

2. Payment Policy

Upfront fare computation based on package duration, overtime hours, and distance thresholds.

  • 50% Advance Required: A mandatory 50% advance deposit is required upon dispatch confirmation to reserve and assign your verified driver.
  • Final Settlement: The remaining 50% balance plus any accrued overtime, night allowance, or return charges must be settled immediately at trip conclusion.
03 CANCELLATIONS
✓ Fair Fee

3. Cancellation Policy

Structured cancellation framework to ensure fair compensation for drivers committing their active time.

  • Free Cancellation: Free cancellation is available before a driver accepts or departs toward your pickup address.
  • Nominal 5% Fee: Once a driver has accepted and is en route, cancellation incurs a standard nominal 5% processing fee on the total booking value to compensate the driver's transit time and fuel.
04 VEHICLE INSURANCE
✓ Mandatory

4. Insurance Information

The platform does not provide primary motor vehicular insurance.

  • Comprehensive Cover: The vehicle owner must maintain active, valid, and comprehensive Indian Motor Tariff insurance (covering third-party liability and own damage) for the dispatched vehicle.
  • Vehicle Insurance Only: Ridingo provides driver services only and does not provide vehicle insurance or damage coverage; only the vehicle owner's own insurance is applicable.
05 TRIP EXPENSES
✓ Owner Cost

5. Additional Costs

Operational on-road expenditures incurred during transit are the customer's direct responsibility.

  • Fuel, Fastag electronic tollway charges, municipal parking fees, state border entry permits, and highway taxes are excluded from the driver service fee.
  • All operational trip fees must be paid directly by the car owner or reimbursed to the driver against official receipts.
06 APP SUPPORT
✓ 24/7 SLA

6. Platform Liability

Ridingo is responsible for digital platform operations, driver background vetting, and secure payment processing.

  • We maintain continuous verification of driver credentials, criminal background checks, and driving license authenticity.
  • Ridingo provides 24/7 in-app emergency assistance, live telemetry tracking, and dispatch dispute mediation.
07 FAIR RESOLUTION
✓ Resolution

7. Dispute Resolution & Grievance

In the rare event of a dispute, our Safety & Compliance Board conducts an objective root-cause inquiry within 48 to 72 business hours.

  • Telemetry records, GPS timestamps, and journey logs are reviewed transparently for dispute settlement.
  • Official grievance contact: teme.ridingo@gmail.com
